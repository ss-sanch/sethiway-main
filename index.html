About Me

Hi, I'm Sanchit.

I am a second-year MORSE (Mathematics, Operational Research, Statistics, and Economics) student at the University of Warwick with a deep-rooted passion for trading and the financial markets.

My journey is driven by a fascination with numbers and how they can be leveraged to understand complex, real-world systems. Whether I am engineering analytical tools like SethiStock or researching market mechanics, my focus is always on translating raw data into a clear, strategic edge.

Beyond academics and the trading desk, I spent years as a competitive swimmer, a background that instilled a relentless work ethic and profound discipline. I am also highly passionate about charity work, and I continually look for ways to leverage my time and skills to give back and support others.

What is the tech stack behind SethiStock?

SethiStock is built on a custom Python backend utilizing Streamlit for the frontend architecture. It routes live market data via the yfinance API and renders dynamic visualizations using Plotly.

Absolutely. I am always looking to expand my technical toolkit and work with other driven developers, quants, and finance professionals. Reach out if you have an opportunity or project in mind.
