How to read this lab

VaR is a loss threshold at a stated confidence level; it is not a guaranteed maximum loss. Expected Shortfall estimates the average loss within the tail beyond that threshold. Historical VaR replays realised market behaviour, while Monte Carlo VaR simulates correlated returns from estimated means and covariance.
