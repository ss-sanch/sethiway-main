Privacy Policy.

Last updated 24 August 2026

Sethiway is a quantitative financial portfolio. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and your choices. It is written to be read, not skimmed past, so it is short and in plain English.

Who we are

Sethiway ("we", "us") operates this website and its sub-projects. For anything privacy-related, please use the contact form on our main hub.

What we collect

  • Contact information: If you use the "Got an Idea" form, we collect the name and email you provide.
  • Usage analytics: We collect basic telemetry (pages viewed, tickers searched) to understand which features are used most. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site trackers.
  • Financial queries: The stock tickers and macroeconomic indicators you search for are processed to fetch data but are not tied to your personal identity.

Who we share it with

Only the infrastructure processors needed to run the service: Web3Forms (for routing your contact messages securely), Render (for our Python backend engines), and API providers that process queries to generate financial data.

Cookies and local storage

We use browser local storage strictly to remember UI preferences. We do not use advertising cookies, which is why there is no intrusive cookie banner.
