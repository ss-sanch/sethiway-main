Black-Scholes Parameters

  • Underlying Price ($S$): The current market price of the asset.
  • Strike Price ($K$): The predetermined price at which the option can be exercised.
  • Days to Expiry ($T$): Time remaining until the contract expires. (The engine automatically converts this to an annualized decimal).
  • Implied Volatility ($\sigma$): The market's theoretical forecast of how much the security's price will move. Higher IV directly inflates option premiums.
  • Risk-Free Rate ($r$): The theoretical baseline return of an investment with zero risk, typically pegged to the US 10-Year Treasury Yield.

The Greeks (Risk Matrix)

  • Delta ($\Delta$): Directional risk. Estimates how much the option's price will change for every $1 move in the underlying asset.
  • Gamma ($\Gamma$): Convexity risk. The rate of change in Delta for a $1 move in the underlying. Highest for at-the-money options.
  • Theta ($\Theta$): Time decay. Measures how much theoretical value the option loses each day simply as time passes, assuming all else is equal.
  • Vega ($\mathcal{V}$): Volatility risk. How much the option's premium will adjust for a 1% change in Implied Volatility.

Modern Portfolio Theory (MPT)

This engine uses SciPy's Sequential Least Squares Programming (SLSQP) algorithm to mathematically calculate the "Tangency Portfolio"—the exact asset allocation that maximizes risk-adjusted returns.

  • Sharpe Ratio: A measure of risk-adjusted return. Formula: $S = \frac{R_p - R_f}{\sigma_p}$. The optimizer runs thousands of simulations to find the highest possible ratio.
  • Expected Return: The weighted average of historical annualized returns.
  • Portfolio Volatility: The overall risk, calculated using the covariance matrix of the assets to account for diversification benefits.
  • Constraints: The algorithm strictly enforces that weights must sum to 100% and prohibits short-selling (no negative weights).

Algorithmic Backtesting

This module tests a rules-based trading strategy against 5 years of historical market data. All signals are shifted by 1 day to strictly eliminate look-ahead bias (you cannot buy at the closing price of the signal day).

  • SMA Crossover: A trend-following strategy. The algorithm generates a "Buy" signal when the Fast Moving Average crosses above the Slow Moving Average (Golden Cross), and sells into cash when it crosses below (Death Cross).
  • Max Drawdown: The largest peak-to-trough drop in the strategy's portfolio value. A critical measure of downside risk.
  • Annualised Volatility: The standard deviation of the strategy's daily returns, scaled to a 252-day trading year to measure the dispersion of returns.
